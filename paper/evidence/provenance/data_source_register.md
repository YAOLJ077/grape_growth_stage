# Data source and reuse register

Terms and source pages checked: **10 September 2026**. “Confirmed” denotes evidence in the retained source metadata or an official provider document. “Recorded” denotes a local acquisition record; it does not establish a provider-wide publication or update date. “Unresolved” denotes information not established by these records. Provider terms remain applicable to the corresponding data; no uniform licence for the combined observations, derived products or repository is established here.

**TEMPO.** Access endpoints are the [TEMPO data portal](https://data.pheno.fr/), its [API](https://data.pheno.fr/api/) and [web-service documentation](https://data.pheno.fr/webservices). The local download manifest records TEMPO acquisition activity on **26 June 2026**, and the API reference identifies the access scope as public, unauthenticated data. The portal reference in the manuscript bibliography is Maury et al. (2023), *Portail de données phénologiques du réseau TEMPO / TEMPO Data Portal*, [doi:10.57745/NQ9HRV](https://doi.org/10.57745/NQ9HRV). The same reference is reproduced in the current official PEP725 policy. This is a portal reference; it is not a versioned DOI for the cleaned study table.

The target records come from TEMPO source 10, Epiphyt (10,950 records; “Licence Ouverte”); source 14, ODS Recherche (1,579 records, including 226 auxiliary-country observations; “Licence Ouverte V2.0”); and source 2, Phenoclim Agroclim INRAE (941 records; “Etalab Open License 2.0”). The metadata links Epiphyt to the [legacy Licence Ouverte document](https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/wp-content/uploads/2014/05/Licence_Ouverte.pdf) and ODS Recherche to [Licence Ouverte 2.0](https://www.etalab.gouv.fr/wp-content/uploads/2017/04/ETALAB-Licence-Ouverte-v2.0.pdf). Their current redirected PDF contents were not retrievable during this check; the recorded licence labels are confirmed, while exact legacy-version wording remains unresolved. TEMPO distributes data under multiple provider licences and gives [provider-specific licensing guidance](https://tempo.pheno.fr/eng/data/licenses).

TEMPO requests acknowledgement of the network and contributing providers, as well as identification of the portal as the download source. Its [official citation guidance](https://tempo.pheno.fr/eng/data) gives the wording. Local metadata distinguishes the accessible Phenoclim Agroclim INRAE source from the separate authorization-dependent Phenoclim Agroclim PRO source; the latter is not identified among the retained target-source groups. Per-dataset DOIs in additional source fields have not been comprehensively reconciled to this target table. Personal permissions or agreements, if any, remain author-held facts.

**DWD.** The direct inputs are the [CDC phenology archive](https://opendata.dwd.de/climate_environment/CDC/observations_germany/phenology/), specifically `annual_reporters/vine/historical/`, `annual_reporters/vine/recent/` and `immediate_reporters/vine/historical/`. The local manifest records downloads on **26 June 2026**, including source URLs and hashes. Retained targets include 16,147 annual-reporter and 2,854 immediate-reporter observations. The [official CDC terms, dated May 2024](https://opendata.dwd.de/climate_environment/CDC/Terms_of_use.pdf), specify CC BY 4.0. Attribution, a licence link and identification of changes are required under the [CC BY 4.0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode). The manuscript reference is Kaspar, Zimmermann and Polte-Rudolf (2014), [doi:10.5194/asr-11-93-2014](https://doi.org/10.5194/asr-11-93-2014). A versioned DOI for this particular direct-download snapshot is not recorded. DWD records obtained through PEP725 retain the separate PEP725 provenance and attribution requirements.

**PEP725.** The local extract `pep725_20260701_0.csv` and its accompanying README explicitly record retrieval on **1 July 2026**. Each row carries `license_short`; the extract is not governed by one uniform noncommercial label. The [current data-use policy](https://pep725.geosphere.at/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/PEP725_Data_Use_Policy.pdf), effective **15 May 2026**, permits redistribution within the applicable record licence. Redistribution outside those terms requires written permission from the data owner or project management. Thus, neither blanket prohibition of redistribution nor unrestricted commercial reuse of the entire extract is supported.

The 4,849 CC BY-NC 4.0 target observations retain the noncommercial condition; sharing and adaptation remain subject to attribution and the other [CC BY-NC 4.0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/). The policy requires the acknowledgement: “Data were provided by the members of the PEP725 project.” It also requires the prescribed Templ et al. (2018) citation, [doi:10.1007/s00484-018-1512-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00484-018-1512-8), with the access date, and requests bibliographic information immediately after publication. No publication notification has been sent. The [current dataset page](https://pep725.eu/dataset/) directs new data requests to project management while direct-download access is being redesigned. The exact permission status of any proposed deposit that changes the record licences remains unresolved.

**ERA5-Land reuse conditions.** The current CDS hourly and monthly catalogues identify the [CC-BY licence](https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/licences/cc-by), whose linked legal text is CC BY 4.0. The GEE daily catalogue additionally retains Copernicus acknowledgement guidance: identify Copernicus Climate Change Service as the source, distinguish modified products, and state that the European Commission and ECMWF are not responsible for downstream use. The applicable upstream terms and attribution should accompany redistributed weather and corrected temperatures. The terrain-corrected series are modified products. The manuscript already contains the hourly dataset reference and Muñoz-Sabater et al. (2021), [doi:10.5194/essd-13-4349-2021](https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-13-4349-2021). The monthly dataset DOI and exact GEE collection identifiers are distinct references. No separate attachment-specific licence statement was found in the local invariant metadata; attribution to the parent ERA5-Land product is established, while any additional attachment-specific terms remain unresolved.

**Copernicus GLO-30 reuse conditions.** The public GLO-30 licence permits reproduction, distribution, public communication, adaptation and combination with other information. Article 6 requires the specified source notice, the modified-product notice where applicable, a liability notice and downstream observance of the obligations; it does not grant ownership of the original data or imply provider endorsement. The relevant text is the **COP-DEM-GLO-30-F** section, printed pages 21–23, of the [ESA mission-specific licence annex](https://s3.waw3-1.cloudferro.com/swift/v1/portal_uploads_prod/CSCDA_ESA_Mission-specific_Annex_31_Oct_22_latest.pdf#page=21). The exact attribution notices are also available in the [official product citation guidance](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/explore-data/data-collections/copernicus-contributing-missions/collections-description/COP-DEM). The applicable instance is public GLO-30, not restricted GLO-30-R or EEA-10.

The AWS distributor identifies its COG collection as originating from the **2021 release**. This does not establish that the files are the latest CDSE release. Its [distribution documentation](https://copernicus-dem-30m.s3.amazonaws.com/readme.html) describes removal of the original bottom and right overlap pixels; source URLs, native transforms and sampled-pixel hashes distinguish the actual inputs. Copernicus DEM is a digital surface model; the site-level terrain metrics and temperature corrections are derived products. The active manuscript now cites the registered Copernicus DEM dataset as European Space Agency and Airbus (2022), DOI 10.5270/ESA-c5d3d65. The citation year does not identify the release of the sampled tiles; [registry evidence](provider_citations_20260911/README.md) preserves the distinction.

## Deposit status and evidence trail

No public deposit URL, accession or DOI for the study-specific observations, weather snapshot, fitted models or code archive is established. Repository visibility and an author-approved licence for original code and other author-owned outputs remain unresolved. The seven-column phenology table omits licence columns, so any permitted record-level redistribution requires accompanying provenance or an equivalent source/licence crosswalk. The noncommercial subset retains its conditions; all source material cannot be relabelled under a single unrestricted licence.
